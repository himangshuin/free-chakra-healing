Frequently Asked Questions

What are the seven chakras?

The seven chakras are the Root (Muladhar), Sacral (Swadhisthana), Solar Plexus (Manipura), Heart (Anhata), Throat (Visuddhi), Brow (Ajna) and Crown (Sahasrara) chakras, running from the base of the spine to the top of the head.

How can I balance my chakras?

Common ways include meditation for chakra healing, chakra balancing affirmations, chakra yoga with sound, changing your diet, and chakra healing Reiki. You can combine them or pick one or two that suit your needs.

What are the signs of a blocked chakra?

Each chakra has its own balanced and unbalanced traits. For example, an unbalanced Root chakra is linked with anxiety, fear and insecurity, while an unbalanced Throat chakra is linked with shyness and fear of speaking. Open any chakra card to see its full list.
